## Brand & Style

This design system targets young, cosmopolitan Malayalis living across Kerala and major NRI hubs (the Gulf, UK, North America, and urban India). The aesthetic bridges contemporary lifestyle culture with a confident, modern sense of place—refusing outdated ethnic tropes in favor of an editorial, warm, and sophisticated design language. 

The emotional tone balances high trust, discretion, social excitement, and effortless cultural familiarity. It conveys the vitality of urban Kochi alongside the relaxed, sunlit, lush environment of the Malabar coast, translated through ultra-clean global product standards.

The visual style blends soft organic minimalism with subtle tactile depth and frosted glass layers:
- **Warm, Tactile Minimalism:** Generous breathing room, deliberate typography, and high-legibility layouts paired with rich, grounded surface tones.
- **Glassmorphic Atmosphere:** Frosted navigation bars, floating action surfaces, and translucent sheet overlays that preserve context while creating depth.
- **Micro-Delight & Reassurance:** High-security visual cues (verified badges, privacy indicators, mutual origin tags) styled gracefully into seamless interface components rather than sterile administrative markers.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from Kerala's natural and coastal landscape without resorting to literal folk motifs:
- **Primary (`#0F3E33`):** Deep Kerala Palm Green. Grounded, sophisticated, and authoritative. Anchors key structural actions, prominent header text, solid identity containers, and active navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`):** Fresh Emerald. Energetic and optimistic. Reserved for dynamic accents, live activity pulses, verified trust badges, primary micro-interactions, and positive event RSVP states.
- **Tertiary (`#F43F5E`):** Soft Rose / Coral. Dedicated to dating, romance intent, warm likes, mutual crushes, and celebration moments. Creates immediate emotional warmth alongside the deep greens.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate/Charcoal. Provides crisp, readable typography with softer contrast than harsh pure black.
- **Background & Canvas:** Crisp, off-white cream surfaces (`#FAF9F6` and `#F3F4F6`) replace sterile clinical white, creating a sunlit editorial paper quality.
- **Accent Highlight:** Subtle warm gold/brass (`#D97706`) utilized sparingly for cultural festival flags, premium memberships, curated NRI mixer spotlights, and top-tier host markers.

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly geometric curves, contemporary look, and open counters. It provides warmth and immediate readability across varying screen densities and multi-script usage contexts.

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked with generous line heights to prevent heavy feeling in warm social interfaces. Used for discovery onboarding, event hero titles, and profile introductions.
- **Body:** Generous 1.6x line-height ratio ensures effortless reading for personal bios, community guidelines, and direct messaging dialogues.
- **Labels & Tags:** Elevated weights (SemiBold to Bold) with positive letter spacing preserve sharp legibility on miniature status pills, hometown origins, and privacy chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for mobile-first social discovery with fluid adaptability across larger screens:
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 428px):** Single-column fluid view with a fixed outer margin of `1.25rem` (20px) and a component gutter of `1rem` (16px). Cards span the full safe width, with horizontal carousels bleeding to the edges using negative margins.
- **Tablet / Responsive Foldables (600px - 840px):** 6-column fluid grid, `1.5rem` margins, dividing social feeds into dual-card masonry or master-detail navigation panes.
- **Desktop / Web Preview (1024px+):** Constrained centered mobile-app frame (maximum width `480px`) flanked by environmental ambient blurs, ensuring focus remains strictly handheld and immediate.

The vertical rhythm utilizes a baseline 4px/8px module. Micro-spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) separates text from contextual status badges, while card groupings and interactive modules depend on `space-md` through `space-xl` to prevent visual fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass layers and low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with the primary palm hue:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FAF9F6` warm base tone with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Lists):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 62, 51, 0.05)`, bounded by a 1px ghost border `rgba(15, 62, 51, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Map Pins):** Slightly lifted elements like active profile cards and markers: `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(15, 62, 51, 0.10)`, with a subtle top rim light `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Bottom Sheets):** `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)` over a dim backdrop of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
- **Level Glass (Header & Tab Bars):** Background `rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a thin bottom border `rgba(15, 62, 51, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a pill-shaped curvature hierarchy (`roundedness: 3`). The generous rounding softens screen edges, encourages tactile thumb interaction, and gives the UI an approachable, modern feel:

- **Base Elements & Badges:** `1rem` (16px) corner radius for small avatars, chips, and micro-tags.
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Medium Cards:** `2rem` (32px) for form inputs, full-width CTA buttons, and interactive chips.
- **Discovery Profile Cards & Hero Panels:** `1.75rem` to `2.25rem` (28px - 36px) for media containers, matching mobile hardware corner profiles.
- **Bottom Sheets:** `2.5rem` (40px) on top-left and top-right radii for sliding preview drawers and filter sheets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Global / Social RSVP):** Deep green (`#0F3E33`) background with crisp white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 52px. Active press state triggers a subtle spring scale down to `0.98`.
- **Dating / Romance Action:** Soft rose (`#F43F5E`) background with white text and a faint glow shadow (`0 8px 20px -2px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Off-white container with a 1px border of `rgba(15, 62, 51, 0.15)` and text in `#0F3E33`.
- **Tactile Floating Action Button (FAB):** 56px circular button with frosted glass reflection and an elevated ambient drop shadow for quick messaging and discovery filters.

### Chips & Badges
- **Hometown & Community Tags (e.g., "Thrissur", "London NRI", "Malayali Tech Meetup"):** Soft tint background (`rgba(15, 62, 51, 0.06)`), primary text `#0F3E33`, 12px horizontal padding, pill-shaped.
- **Dating Intent Badges:** Pale rose tint (`#FFF1F2`) with `#F43F5E` label text and mini lead icons.
- **Privacy & Verification Indicators:** Clean emerald (`#10B981`) checkmark icon encased within a micro pill badge with white typography.

### Profile Cards & Discovery Stack
- Multi-layered card with `rounded-3xl` corners. Edge-to-edge photography with a gentle bottom scrim gradient (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` to transparent).
- Integrated info overlays: Name, Age, Hometown, Current City (with flight icon for diaspora/NRI context), and verified audio prompt badge.

### Map Pins & Location Markers
- Circular user avatars encased in a 3px white ring and primary green teardrop pin stem.
- Micro-pill category tags floating above pins indicating live event count (e.g., "Onam Mixer · 14 attendees").

### Bottom Sheets & Previews
- Draggable sheets with a centered pill grab-handle (width 36px, height 4px, `#CBD5E1`).
- Contains user profile previews, community privacy filters (e.g., "Hide from family/relatives in Kerala"), and event ticket confirmation flows.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Large 54px touch targets with pill borders.
- Background in solid `#FFFFFF`, text in `#0F172A`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state shifts border color to `#10B981` with an ambient glow of `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Generous item height with 16px vertical padding, separated by soft hair lines (`rgba(15, 62, 51, 0.06)`).
- Avatars with active green presence dots and mutual connection link text (e.g., "Mutual with 3 friends in Kozhikode").
