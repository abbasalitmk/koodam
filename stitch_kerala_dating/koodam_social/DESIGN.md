## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking 2026 mobile-first aesthetic rooted in warm editorial sensibility and uncompromising physical-world safety. Built to connect the global Malayali diaspora through high-trust local meetups, intimate cultural gatherings, and authentic dating, the design avoids transient tech gimmicks in favor of grounded richness, organic geometry, and quiet luxury.

The personality balances three distinct emotional registers:
1. **Sanctuary & Safety:** Anchored by botanical depth and verifiable accountability. Visual cues emphasize verified community standing, vouched profiles, and safe public meeting spaces.
2. **Warmth & Conviviality:** Evocative of late-evening porch conversations (*thinnas*), celebratory feasts, and thoughtful romantic intent, rendered through warm clay, sunlight amber, and unbleached cream tones.
3. **Progressive Modernity:** Ultra-refined typography, generous macro-whitespace, precision edge-radii, and micro-tactile surface treatments that place it at par with world-class editorial products.

The design movement merges **Tactile Editorial** with **Warm Organic Minimalism**. Layouts rely on ample breathing room, expansive full-bleed cards, lush photographic frames with subtle warm film grain, and deliberate pill-shaped tactile controls.

## Colors

The palette draws deeply from Kerala’s indigenous visual landscape—monsoon flora, baked clay tiles, and temple brass—interpreted through high-contrast digital utility.

### Color Tokens & Semantic Assignments

- **Primary (`#0A382C` - Deep Nilgiri Emerald):** The foundational brand color. Communicates legacy, grounded security, and calm discernment. Applied to primary CTAs, active global navigation, key headers, and core verification watermarks.
- **Secondary (`#FF5A5F` - Terracotta Coral):** The emotional catalyst. Used selectively for intentional dating signals, match confirmations, emotional reactions, like states, and romantic meetups. It never overpowers the primary emerald but sparks against it.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Marigold Warm Gold):** The beacon of trust. Reserved for 3-peer community verifications, "Vouched" trust badges, VIP event passes, and micro-moments of recognition.
- **Neutral Dark (`#121816` - Deep Palm Bark):** Deepest reading tier for titles and primary interactive labels, carrying an ultra-subtle green-black bias rather than stark neutral black.
- **Neutral Muted (`#64748B` - Slate Smoke):** Secondary body copy, timestamps, metadata labels, trailing icons, and passive divider outlines.
- **Background Base (`#FBFBF9` - Coconut Pearl):** A bespoke unbleached warm white canvas that eliminates display harshness, offering a paper-like editorial reading experience.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Card White):** Crisp white reserved for primary interactive cards, overlays, and elevated bottom sheets to cleanly dissociate from the pearl foundation.
- **Success & Trust (`#0D5F48`):** Harmonious emerald variant for mutual consent indicators and identity checkmarks.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy is powered by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, modern humanistic terminals, and generous x-height. This ensures crystal clarity across bi-lingual Latin and Malayalam scripts.

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked with negative letter spacing to project deliberate authority and magazine-like presence. Used for profile introductions, curated event titles, and milestone greetings.
- **Body:** Open, relaxed tracking optimized for high-legibility bio reading, host notes, and neighborhood descriptions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Crisp medium-to-bold weights with slight positive tracking for chips, verification metadata, and interactive button text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces a mobile-first fluid single-column structure that systematically adapts into a dual/triple modular feed across tablet and desktop viewpoints.

### Grid Rhythm & Screen Breakpoints
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single column, strict 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margin, 16px (`1rem`) horizontal element gutter. Dynamic top safe areas and 96px padded bottom navigation clearance.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid grid, 32px (`2rem`) margins, 20px gutters. Dual-pane layout for split discovery and profile drill-down.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column fixed grid with a max-width container of 1180px, flanked by auto side-margins. Split into 3 columns: Left fixed navigation (280px), Center discovery stream (620px), Right contextual event details and peer vouching rail (280px).

### Spacing Cadence
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal pill padding, icon-to-label gaps, and verification badge clusters.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for card body padding and stack margins between related metadata.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for full container padding on hero cards and section separation.
- Use `space-xl` (36px) exclusively between major visual sections, such as passing from "Featured Koodam Meetups" to "Profiles in Your Circle."

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through organic surface layering and warm ambient shadow geometry rather than hard-edged artificial elevation.

### Spatial Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBFBF9` acts as the silent, unreactive plane.
- **Interactive Planes (Level 1):** Floating event and profile cards use pure `#FFFFFF` resting against the canvas, backed by a soft, diffused shadow tinted with Nilgiri green:
  `0 8px 28px -4px rgba(10, 56, 44, 0.06), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 56, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Raised & Draggable Panels (Level 2):** Bottom sheets, peer vouching dialogs, and filters use `#FFFFFF` elevated with:
  `0 20px 48px -10px rgba(18, 24, 22, 0.12), 0 4px 14px -2px rgba(18, 24, 22, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Overlays (Level 3):** Verification approval prompts and matching animations feature:
  `0 32px 64px -12px rgba(10, 56, 44, 0.20)`.

### Border Treatment
Elevated elements utilize an ultra-subtle "hairline ghost border" instead of heavy lines: `1px solid rgba(10, 56, 44, 0.06)`. This locks edge integrity over warm imagery and light-toned wallpapers.

## Shapes

The design system employs an ultra-rounded, soft structural vocabulary that invites physical touch and conveys safety:

- **Macro Containers (`rounded-3xl` / 28px - 32px):** Primary event modules, meetup passes, interactive profile discovery cards, and elevated bottom sheets.
- **Interactive Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** All actionable buttons, interest tags, verification markers, attendee counters, and floating tab navigators.
- **Inner Framing (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):** Embedded images inside cards, map preview snippets, and peer avatar frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Join Koodam / RSVP):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), solid `#0A382C`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`. Height 52px. Hover/active shifts to `#06241C`.
- **Secondary Romantic / Match Action:** Full pill, solid `#FF5A5F`, foreground `#FFFFFF`. Pressed state scales gently down (0.97 scale factor).
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Full pill, transparent background, `#0A382C` text with a 1px border of `rgba(10, 56, 44, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **3-Peer Vouched Badge:** Pill-shaped, background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.14)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`. Left-anchored with a solid gold shield icon.
- **Category & Interest Chips:** Pill-shaped, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#64748B`, border `1px solid rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.18)`. When selected: background `#0A382C`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-color transparent.

### Cards
- **Discovery Profile Card:** Bound by `rounded-3xl`, `#FFFFFF` surface, wrapped in the signature green-tinted shadow. Features 3:4 aspect ratio hero imagery with an integrated linear fade to pure black at the base for white text legibility. Verified badges float top-right on an frosted pill layer (`backdrop-blur-md`).
- **Event Meetup Pass Card:** Designed with editorial ticket proportions. Houses event date in a compact vertical emerald stamp, host vouching avatars, and a dedicated RSVP pill at the base.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Text Input:** 52px pill or `rounded-2xl` surface with background `#FFFFFF` and border `1.5px solid rgba(10, 56, 44, 0.12)`. Active focus transitions border to `#0A382C` with a concentric 3px ambient ring of `rgba(10, 56, 44, 0.08)`. Placeholder set in `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Verification Agreement Checkboxes:** Smooth `rounded-lg` (8px), emerald border. Checked state fills with `#0A382C` displaying a crisp white micro-tick.
- **Gender & Intent Radios:** Custom segmented pill selectors rather than naked radio dots; active selection triggers an animated pill slide with spring tension.

### Specialized Platform Components
- **Peer-Vouch Indicator:** Stacked triad avatar ring with overlapping -8px margins, framed in `#FFFFFF` 2px strokes, culminating in a `+3 Vouched` gold-toned tag.
- **Safety Beacon Sheet:** A persistent, non-intrusive safety control accessible on any active meetup screen, styled in soft rose-tinted neutral tones.
